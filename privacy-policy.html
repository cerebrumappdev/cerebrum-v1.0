Cerebrum App Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Cerebrum is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

  • No Personal Accounts: We do not require user registration or account creation
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, emails, or personal identifiers
  • App Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics to improve app performance
  • Device Information: Basic device info (Android version, screen size) for compatibility

2. How We Use Information

The limited data we collect is used solely to:

  • Provide cryptocurrency news and market data
  • Deliver AI-powered chat functionality
  • Send price alerts and notifications
  • Improve app performance and user experience

3. Third-Party Services

Cerebrum uses the following third-party services:

  • DeepSeek AI: For chat functionality (no personal data shared)
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements
  • Firebase: For app analytics and crash reporting
  • News APIs: For cryptocurrency and financial news

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device:

  • Chat Messages: Not stored permanently - cleared when app closes
  • Alert Settings: Stored locally on your device
  • App Preferences: Stored locally on your device

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties except:

  • Anonymous analytics data with Google Firebase
  • Ad serving data with Google AdMob
  • AI chat requests with DeepSeek (no personal identifiers)

6. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, you have full control:

  • No Account Required: No data to delete or modify
  • Local Control: Clear app data anytime through device settings
  • Uninstall: Remove all data by uninstalling the app

7. Children's Privacy

Cerebrum is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
